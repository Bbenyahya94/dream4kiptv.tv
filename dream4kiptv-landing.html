OFFICIAL KNOWLEDGE BASE

Common setup questions.

How do I set up my DNS?

After activation, use the DNS details supplied to your account and enter them in your compatible app or device settings. Contact support if your device requires a specific method.

Can I use M3U or Xtream API login?

Supported apps can use the credentials supplied after activation. The exact login method depends on your app and the plan you select.

How are panel credits managed?

Authorized resellers manage credits, customer lines, and sub-reseller access in the assigned reseller panel. Ask your account admin about credit allocation rules.

How do server renewals work?

Renew before your displayed expiry date through your account workflow or support channel. Keeping your contact information current helps avoid an interruption.
